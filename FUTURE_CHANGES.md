# FindBack - Future Improvements

**Current pilot account setup:** The school head creates up to 10 student accounts. Students sign in with their school ID and password. Open self-sign-up is disabled.

## Future goals

These ideas can be considered after the 10-account pilot has been tried with demo item data.

1. **Ready-made account batches**
   - Replace the pilot limit with school-approved account batches when the school is ready.
   - Keep account creation restricted to the authorized school head or administrator.
   - Provide a simple way to disable an account when access should end.

2. **Account help**
   - Add an administrator-assisted password reset process for students who lose access.
   - Provide clear school-approved support contact details.
   - Keep any recovery method aligned with the school’s account policy.

3. **Staff account maintenance**
   - Add safe account disable/remove controls for the authorized school head.
   - Keep invigilator access changes restricted to the head.
   - Review staff access periodically and remove it when no longer needed.

4. **School rollout**
   - Confirm the approved student roster and who may manage accounts.
   - Get school approval before storing real student or item information.
   - Review Supabase and hosting limits, privacy needs, and backup expectations before wider use.

## Future acceptance checklist

- Students cannot create accounts or grant themselves staff access.
- Only the school head or an authorized administrator can create or disable accounts.
- A student cannot access another student's profile, private claim, or answers.
- Password help follows the school-approved recovery process.
- Invigilator and head actions remain restricted to their assigned roles.
- The wider rollout uses only the school-approved roster and privacy rules.
